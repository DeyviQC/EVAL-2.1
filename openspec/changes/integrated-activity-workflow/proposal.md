# Integrated activity workflow (approved block 3)
One Activities navigation entry: classroom > course > activity > deliveries > assessment.
Retain AD/A/B/C, teacher feedback, authenticated access, version history, notifications and uncertain-write recovery.
Teachers filter authorized course choices by grade and section before pagination, with all authorized classroom metadata.
A teacher-only paginated pending-student projection uses enrollment eligibility at publication or current active enrollment in an active period. Historical pending students remain visible for traceability; this is not a claim that they can submit today.
Students never access the pending roster or other students' deliveries.
Deadline badges are informative and do not close submission access. Confirm success only after a valid backend receipt.
No schema change. Activity edit/delete, calendar synchronization and trimester dates remain later approval blocks.
