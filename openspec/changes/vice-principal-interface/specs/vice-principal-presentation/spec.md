## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Limit the presentation change to Vice Principal content

The presentation SHALL apply only to the main workspace under the existing vice_principal role marker. The sidebar, header, mobile navigation, other roles and business permissions SHALL remain unchanged.

### Requirement: Provide clear borderless controls

Secondary buttons SHALL have a pale blue borderless background, distinct hover and pressed states, visible keyboard focus and disabled feedback. Existing important blue actions and selected controls SHALL retain their strong color hierarchy. Text fields, selectors and textareas SHALL use a bottom line that turns blue on focus without a perimeter border. Checkbox, radio, hidden and file inputs SHALL retain their specialized behavior.

### Requirement: Improve readability and import presentation

Cards and forms SHALL use more spacing and soft shadows instead of perimeter borders. Light/dark themes, reduced motion and narrow screens SHALL remain supported. Import controls SHALL use existing vector icons with text labels instead of emoji. Status colors and table separators SHALL remain meaningful.

### Requirement: Present a visual Vice Principal home

The home SHALL provide a school-themed decorative illustration, a personalized welcome, the existing live academic overview and shortcuts to existing authorized workspaces. No statistics SHALL be invented. The illustration SHALL require no external network asset and SHALL be hidden from assistive technology when decorative. Teacher, Director and Student home renderers SHALL remain unchanged.
